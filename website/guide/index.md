# What is Photokin?

Photokin is a free desktop app for browsing, organizing and rediscovering the
photos and videos you keep on your own computer. It is **local-first**: your
library lives on your device, with no account, no cloud upload and no tracking.

![Photokin showing a photo library](/screenshots/01-library.webp){.screenshot}

## Who it is for

- **Everyone with a folder full of photos.** Add the folders you already have
  and Photokin turns them into a timeline, a calendar, a list of the places you
  visited, and the people in them. You don't need to know what "EXIF" means.
- **Photographers and archivists.** Photokin reads and writes the same
  standard metadata (EXIF, IPTC and XMP) as professional tools, shows exactly
  where every value comes from, and never writes to your files unless you ask it
  to.

## The ideas behind it

**Your folders stay the source of truth.** Photokin indexes photos where they
already are. It doesn't import them into a hidden library, so your folders keep
working with every other app and backup tool.

**Everything runs on your computer.** Face recognition and smart search use
models that run locally. A few optional features use the internet (maps,
downloading a search model, updates), and the [privacy page](/guide/privacy)
lists every one of them.

**You decide what happens to your files.** By default, tags, ratings and other
changes stay inside Photokin's library. When you're ready, you can have them
written into the photos themselves, in standard formats other apps can read.

## What Photokin is not

- **Not a backup.** Photokin works with your photos where they are. Keep your
  own backups, especially before letting any app write to your originals.
- **Not a cloud service.** There is no sync between devices or sharing link.
- **Not a RAW editor.** Photokin offers simple edits (crop, rotate, resize).
  Camera RAW files aren't supported yet.
